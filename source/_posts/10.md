按路径打开 并修改nginx配置：/home/wwwroot/lnmp01/vhost/你的域名.conf
找到：
`location ~ .*\.php$`

修改为：

``
location ~ .*\.php(\/.*)*$
``

按路径打开 并修改重写规则：/home/wwwroot/lnmp01/rewrite/amh.conf

``
if (-f $request_filename/index.php){
    rewrite (.*) $1/index.php;
}
if (!-f $request_filename){
    rewrite (.*) /index.php;
}
``
